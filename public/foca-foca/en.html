01 · Our story

A focacceria made by two pairs of hands.

Gabriele brings Italian tradition and fifteen years of baking and pizza-making experience. Janainy adds her gift for hospitality and creating a place where you want to linger.

100% natural Italian flours, dough prepared daily and more than 24 hours of slow fermentation. Cured meats and cheeses chosen for their quality. More flavour, more aroma, more lightness.

A little piece of Italy, now in the heart of Setúbal.
